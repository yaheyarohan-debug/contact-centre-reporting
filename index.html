Fairfirst New Recruits Training Manual (2026)

Purpose of this manual:

To provide new recruits with a practical foundation in insurance, the Sri Lankan insurance environment, Fairfirst's business context, contact-centre responsibilities, and customer-handling standards.

1. Key Insurance Terminology

  • Insurer: Fairfirst Insurance Limited
  • Premium: The amount paid or payable for insurance cover.
  • Sum Insured: Stated amount used as the basis of cover.
  • Deductible / Excess: Portion of loss borne by insured.
  • Hotline: 0112 428 428

2. Principles & Regulation (IRCSL & Ombudsman)

Operates under the Regulation of Insurance Industry Act No. 43 of 2000 supervised by IRCSL. Dispute escalations follow internal resolution before the Insurance Ombudsman Scheme.

3. Fairfirst CQM & Quality Standards

Contact center quality monitoring weighs active listening (10%), courtesy (20%), script adherence (10%), and product knowledge (10%). Target score: Above 90 (Excellent), 80-90 (Good).
